# Residential Lease Agreement

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the Landlord and the Tenant
for the property described below. The parties agree to the terms set out in the numbered clauses.

1. Parties and Property

The Landlord leases to the Tenant the apartment located at Kralja Petra 12, Belgrade ("Premises").
The Premises are let for residential use only and may not be used for any commercial activity.

2. Term

The lease term is twelve (12) months, commencing on 1 March 2026 and ending on 28 February 2027.
The term renews automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives notice
under Clause 5.

3. Rent

Monthly rent is EUR 850, due on the first day of each calendar month. Rent is paid by bank transfer
to the account designated by the Landlord. A late fee of EUR 25 applies to any payment received more
than five (5) days after the due date.

4. Security Deposit

The Tenant pays a security deposit of EUR 1,700 before taking possession. The Landlord returns the
deposit within thirty (30) days after the Tenant vacates, less any deductions for unpaid rent or
damage beyond ordinary wear and tear.

5. Termination

The Tenant shall give sixty (60) days' written notice before terminating this lease. The Landlord may
terminate for material breach — including rent unpaid for more than fifteen (15) days — by giving
thirty (30) days' written notice. Notice is effective when delivered to the address on record.

6. Maintenance and Repairs

The Tenant keeps the Premises clean and reports needed repairs promptly. The Landlord is responsible
for structural repairs and for the heating, plumbing, and electrical systems, except where damage is
caused by the Tenant's negligence.

7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Serbia. Any dispute that cannot be resolved
amicably shall be submitted to the competent court in Belgrade.
